# NEMESIS × Telegraph — demo script

Target: 85 seconds. ~205 words at a natural pace. Read it slightly slower than
feels right; the visuals need room to land.

---

### 1 · The problem (0:00–0:10)

> When your wallet gets drained, you know one thing: your address.
> You don't know which transaction was the theft. Every tool asks you anyway.

**On screen:** landing page, then the wallet input with an address being pasted.

---

### 2 · Wallet only (0:10–0:24)

> NEMESIS starts from the wallet. It reviews the outgoing history, picks the
> strongest candidate, and verifies that transaction directly on the blockchain.
> When the evidence is close, it says so — a likely incident, with real
> confidence, not a guess dressed up as proof.

**On screen:** case opening, then the amber "Likely incident identified · 42%
selection confidence" panel.

---

### 3 · The trace (0:24–0:36)

> Then it follows the money. Every hop is verified, persisted, and kept under
> watch — including the branches that go quiet.

**On screen:** Fund trace section, graph and branches.

---

### 4 · Telegraph, automatically (0:36–0:54)

> Here's what NEMESIS can't do alone. It can prove what happened on the chain.
> It can't know what the rest of the world already knows.
> So when a verified event needs outside context, NEMESIS buys it — routing to a
> live Telegraph miner and paying over x402. No button. No human.

**On screen:** Overview's Telegraph block, then clicking through to the
Telegraph Intelligence section.

---

### 5 · The proof (0:54–1:10)

> One cent of USDC, settled on Base Sepolia, with the transaction and the signal
> hash kept as proof. You can open the settlement on a block explorer from
> inside the case.

**On screen:** a receipt, then "View payment proof" expanded, then the explorer
link opening.

---

### 6 · The part that matters (1:10–1:25)

> And NEMESIS doesn't just trust what it paid for. One miner told us an address
> was flagged as high risk. It wasn't an address — it was a transaction hash,
> and the answer came from a language model's memory.
> So every paid response is validated against verified evidence before it counts
> as intelligence. Miners add context. They never overwrite the chain.

**On screen:** the "no case-specific signal" state, then the accepted/purchased
counts on Overview.

---
